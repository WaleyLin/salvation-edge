Rules for everyone

  • Max 3 Resonance stacks. A 4th kills you.
  • Your stack becomes the last shape you pick up. Closers: make your last pickup the shape your conduit needs.
  • Plates: step on only when the orange circle shows, then step off. Too early or late breaks the chain and spawns a Subjugator.
  • Resonance has a timer (about 90 seconds). Don't grab it early and wait around.
  • Closer = shoots the conduit and locks it. Sender/Back plate = bounces energy and clears adds. Ad clear = kills enemies and collects Resonance.
