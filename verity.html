4. Verity

Plan: 3 people always get sent inside. We choose 2 of them: Daniel and Jake, the two furthest from the start. The 3rd is one of the other four (Waley, Jade, Jordan or Kenneth), and we don't pick who.

Jordan/KennethOutside, ghosts and adds. Suggested: you two bring the ghosts back (see below), and clear adds. If one of you is inside, the other carries ghosts, and Waley helps when not dissecting. Change it if you want.

Bringing ghosts back to their guardians

Before you start: everyone uses a different ghost shell and different looking armor. The statues look like each of you, and you need to tell whose ghost is whose.

When it happens: a message says the Witness notices your efforts, and players "die". This happens several times:

  1. While the inside players are in: they die. Their ghosts are at the back of the room.
  2. After everyone is back outside: the Witness kills most of the team and leaves one alive. That player revives everyone else, and revived players then help.

How to bring one back:

  1. Pick up the dead player's ghost from the back of the room.
  2. Deposit it in that player's statue. Do it in order left to right.
  3. Dead players can only see other people's statues, not their own. So they spectate and call out which statue to use. A simple way is to number the statues 1 to 6 from left to right and call "Name, number".
A wrong statue or wrong order can cause trouble. My sources disagree on how bad (one says it revives you in the wrong place, another says it kills you), so go slowly and double-check the call.

Everyone repeats this cycle 3 times.
