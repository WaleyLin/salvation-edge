5. Zenith (The Witness)

Goal: break all 6 glyphs on his chest, then damage him. Repeat until final stand.

Weapons

High precision damage.

WaleyGlyphbreaker for the Triangle hand (ceiling, green, triangle zone).
JadeGlyphbreaker for the Circle hand (floor, yellow, circle zone).
JakeGlyphbreaker for the Square hand (wall, red, long line zone).
DanielTrap person. Shoots the crystals during "The Witness tests you."
JordanAd clear, left side. Kills Subjugators and Screebs.
KennethAd clear, right side. Kills Subjugators and Screebs.

Glyphbreaker steps (Waley/Jade/Jake)

  1. Pre-aim at your hand's bangle. Step into its zone, shoot the bangle, step out immediately (staying gives you a useless stack).
  2. The bangle shows a colour and shape. It is never your own. Say it shows circle.
  3. Go to that shape's zone on the floor and stand until you get a stack of it. No shooting.
  4. Go back to your hand, step in, shoot the bangle, step out. You now have Glyphbreaker.
  5. Run to the island in front of the Witness and shoot a glyph on his chest.
  6. Your hand respawns elsewhere. Repeat until all 6 are broken.

The trap (Daniel)

  1. When you see "The Witness tests you," crystals appear in the center.
  2. Glyphbreakers call out which hand glowed yellow and screeched when it broke. Up/circle hand = circle, side/square hand = square, down/triangle hand = triangle.
  3. Daniel shoots every crystal outside that shape. A wrong shot kills you.
  4. Everyone jumps when the Witness's eyes glow and you hear the swish.

Damage and final stand

  1. After the last glyph, two Subjugators spawn. Jordan (left) and Kenneth (right) kill them immediately.
  2. Everyone damages him. Stay on the safe half of the platform and jump on the eyes-glow cue.
  3. When he lowers the platform, everyone jumps back to the main arena right away.
  4. At the final stand, push all damage. You have about 15 to 20 seconds.
