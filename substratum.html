1. Substratum

Goal: lock 3 sets of conductors before the wipe timer ends (it starts at 4:15). No boss.

Weapons

Bring add clear weapons and survivability. You'll often be alone in a room, so anything that heals you helps, like Devour or a healing grenade.

Teams: two teams of 3. The two doors that open are one wall door and one corner door.

Corner team (takes the corner door)
WaleyCloser. Shoots the conduit and locks it.
JadeSender. Stands on the lit plate and starts each bounce.
JordanAd clear. Kills enemies, helps both plates, collects 3 Resonance in the center.

Step by step

  1. Start: everyone in the center. Anyone picks up the orange triangle and dunks it in the box. Back off the doors for a second, since exploding Supplicants come out.
  2. Laps (all 6): each team goes through its door and clears rooms. Kill the Minotaur, then the Hydra on the other side, which opens doors. An Overload Minotaur appears in a third room.
  3. When the Overload dies, a Tormentor spawns in the center. When you see the message calling you back, both teams run to the center and kill it together.
  4. Do 2 laps. Then each team has one glowing plate (in the room where its Overload died).
  5. Plates: Sender stands by the lit plate. Closer finds the room with the open conduit and stands on its plate. Ad clear floats.
  6. Sender steps on. When the energy arrives, Closer steps on when the circle shows. Keep bouncing, grabbing Resonance between steps.
  7. Get 3 stacks each. Ad clear collects theirs in the center.
  8. Lock (or when about 40 seconds remain): Closer shoots the glowing Crux on the conduit, then steps on the plate as the energy arrives. Closer loses one stack.
  9. Both teams locked: everyone goes to the center, kills the Subjugator, then dunks all Resonance in the box (about +20 seconds each).
  10. Repeat until 3 sets are locked.
Watch for: Weavers pulling people off plates (kill them first). Orange triangle zones on the floor. Never grab a 4th stack.
