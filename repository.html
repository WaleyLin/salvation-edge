3. Repository

Goal: go through 3 rooms. In each, lock 3 conductors, dunk, and kill an Unstoppable Champion to open the next door.

Weapons

Sleeper Simulant and a sniper, plus add clear.

  • Sleeper Simulant: heavy damage on Tormentors and the Unstoppable Champion.
  • Sniper: Whisper of the Worm or any precision sniper. Use it on the Scorpius turrets in high spots.
  • Add clear: Tarrabah or Riskrunner for the waves between bounces.

Teams: same pairs as Dissipation.

WaleyCloser, with Jade.
DanielCloser, with Kenneth.
JakeCloser, with Jordan.
Jade/Kenneth/JordanStarter plate: bounce energy, kill adds and the Scorpius turrets in high spots between bounces.

Step by step (each room)

  1. Start: someone dunks the starting Resonance. Three Tormentors spawn, one per pair.
  2. Closers must land the final blow on their Tormentor. Partners can help, but let the closer get the last hit. That gives the closer the Stolen Valor buff, which shows the needed shape.
  3. Closer calls out the shape. Bounce and collect Resonance.
  4. Closer's last pickup is the needed shape. Shoot the conductor, then step on the plate to lock.
  5. All 3 locked: the chest appears in the middle. Everyone dunks.
  6. An Unstoppable Champion spawns at the back. Kill it and the door opens.
Rooms 2 and 3: wires are crossed, so plates feed a different conductor. Call it out. Room 3: the encounter ends when the last Champion dies, so you can lock early with 1 or 2 stacks.
