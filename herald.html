2. Dissipation (Herald of Finality)

Goal: lock all 3 pillars, deposit Resonance to drop the Herald's immunity, deal damage, and repeat until the final stand.

Weapons

Swords.

  • Heavy slot (damage): Falling Guillotine, Ergo Sum, or Lament.
  • Back plate add clear: Tarrabah or Riskrunner to clear Taken Knights off the plates.
  • Closers: keep an add-clear primary too, since waves keep coming between bounces.

Teams: three pairs, one per area (left, middle, right). Pick your areas before you start.

Jade Kenneth Jordan are the back plate: they start each bounce, clear adds, and make sure the closer has the shape they need.

Step by step

  1. Someone shoots the Taken blight in the middle to start. Don't let Taken Knights stand on plates.
  2. Each pair clears waves until a Trammel (boss Taken) appears. Kill it to power your plates.
  3. One person per side gets called to the center (Call to Reckoning). Those 3 shoot the Herald's head and then the blights. When his head pops they get Stolen Favor. They must stay until it converts, then go back to their side.
  4. Whoever has Stolen Favor looks at your side's conduit and calls out the shape (circle or triangle).
  5. Back plate and closer bounce the energy. After each bounce, triangles spawn on one side of the arena and spheres on the other.
  6. Closer: make your last pickup the called shape. Back plate: grab anything, but leave at least one of the needed shape for your closer.
  7. Closer shoots the conduit, then steps on the plate as the energy arrives. If you miss, the Trammel returns and the conduit symbol may change, so look again.
  8. All 3 locked: the box appears by the Herald. Everyone deposits. Then everyone damages him until he goes immune.
  9. Repeat. At the final stand he calls the three Trammels. Ignore them and keep shooting him.
